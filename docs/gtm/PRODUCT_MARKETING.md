# Product marketing context (SGPS:GTM, ASK-GTM)

The context file the Marketing Skills pack reads in place of `.agents/product-marketing.md`. It is a pointer to this repository's truth, not a second source: when the two disagree, the truth files win.

- **What this site is:** the public house site of BlueSkyz Labs at `https://blueskyzlabs.com` (ADR 0006). It presents the house and its products; it is not a payment, signing or store authority (Owner D-0, `AGENTS.md`).
- **Languages:** vi and en; the same meaning in both (GRB, `docs/editorial/PUBLIC_CONTENT_STANDARD.md`).
- **Who reads it:** people deciding whether to trust and try a BlueSkyz product (for example landlords for Sổ Trọ), partners and press.
- **Products and their facts:** only what the public registry and product-truth files publish (`src/data/`, `docs/current-work.json`, `docs/evidence/product-source-qualification.json`). Unpublished products are never named.

## Claims we can prove

Only claims backed by the claim and truth gates in this repository. Copy that needs a new claim records the proof in the PR first.
