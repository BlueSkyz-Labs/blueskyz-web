A receipt lets the tenant see what they are paying for, and gives the landlord a record when the two need to compare. A usable one has the parts below.

## What the receipt needs

1. The house name, the room number and the tenant's name (a name they are known by is fine).
2. The period: month, year and the date the readings were taken.
3. Each charge: rent; electricity (old reading, new reading, units used, unit price, amount); water (the same); rubbish, internet and parking if they apply.
4. The total due, in figures with the currency (đồng).
5. The due date and how to pay: cash or bank transfer, with the account number or a QR code.
6. A note: any unpaid balance from the previous period.

## A template to fill in

Room …… · Month ……/…… · Readings taken on ……

| Charge                     | Detail                                                                 | Amount |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| Rent                       |                                                                        | …… VND |
| Electricity                | old …… → new ……, used …… kWh × the unit price in the tenancy agreement | …… VND |
| Water                      | old …… → new ……, used …… m³ × the unit price in the tenancy agreement  | …… VND |
| Rubbish, internet, parking |                                                                        | …… VND |
| Total                      |                                                                        | …… VND |

The electricity and water price charged to a tenant follows the tenancy agreement and the rules in force. Check those rules before you apply a price.

## How to send it

Send it through Zalo with a payment QR code so the tenant can pay straight away, then note the date you received the money. A closed receipt should not be edited quietly. If something changes, add a correction line that both sides can see.
