YENLUME (say _yen-loom_) is a rent book from BlueSkyz Labs for boarding-house landlords in Vietnam. This post explains the problem it was made for, what it does today and which plans it has.

## The end of the month in a row of rooms

Picture a landlord in her sixties. At the end of the month she sits down with a school exercise book, a pocket calculator and a few hurried photos of the electricity meters. She adds up rent, electricity, water and rubbish for each room, then messages each tenant. One wrong meter number means an unpleasant phone call. Her children live far away and want to help, but they cannot see how far her book has got. (This is an illustrative scene, not a specific customer.)

YENLUME is made for people like her, with one rule: if an older person cannot use it alone, it is not finished.

## What YENLUME does

- **Itemised receipts.** Rent, electricity, water and other charges each have their own line, and meter readings carry their units. The tenant sees what they are paying for.
- **VietQR and Zalo.** The receipt carries a VietQR transfer code and can be sent through Zalo.
- **One close for the whole row.** Enter each room's readings, then close the month in one go. Sending receipts for the whole row starts at the Sân plan; the Hiên plan sends room by room.
- **Review before saving.** The final confirmation step repeats the amount, the room and the way it was collected.
- **Closed books stay closed.** A correction goes in as a separate line that both sides can see.
- **Large text, big buttons.** There is a light and a dark mode, and the interface works in Vietnamese, English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文.

The web version is in preview and accepts new workspaces in limited numbers. The Android and iOS apps are in development and are not in the app stores yet. The screenshots come from a test build with sample data.
